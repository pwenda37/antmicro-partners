Good to know

  • Variant toolbar at the bottom of the Partners page switches Hero, Hero color, Grid layout, Figma export and Partner preview. Press H to hide it.
  • Light / dark theme: use the moon icon in the site header.
  • Internet needed: fonts and global site styles load live from antmicro.com.
  • Prototype only: the contact form validates but does not send anything.
